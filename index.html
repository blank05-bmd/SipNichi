Our story

A little blue-and-cream room, day by day.

Nichi Nichi — にちにち, “day by day” — is a small café for the hours between things: the coffee before the next errand, the matcha after a long day, the slow afternoon by the window.

Pull up a rattan chair, browse the shelf of manga and toys, and stay a while.
